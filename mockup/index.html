Frame Setup

Put Frame Home on a Lifelong Frameo photo frame, one step at a time

Bundled: Frame Home 0.48 · adb 36.0.0

Good to know

  • ADB gets locked again. Frameo clears this PC's ADB access every time the frame restarts or Frameo comes to the front. Fix: turn ADB access off and on again in Frameo settings.
  • "failed to read copy response: EOF" sometimes appears when installing. The wizard retries once by itself.
  • The wizard never taps the frame's screen. ADB taps can register as a long-press on these frames, so you do the tapping yourself.
  • No system files are changed. Frame Home is a normal app install; nothing under /system is touched.
  • After a restart Frame Home takes about 4–5 minutes to appear. The frame's software blocks quick auto-start, so Frame Home brings itself back with a timer.
Mockup only, not part of the app: show this step as Click any step on the left. The buttons inside the window also work (Connect, Scan, Restart ADB, Install, Restart test…).
